🪜 Bucket Laddering Simulator

A smart cash-flow protection strategy that insulates your retirement from stock market crashes (Sequence of Returns Risk).

How the Bucket Strategy Works

Instead of keeping all your retirement money in one giant mixed portfolio, you divide it into distinct buckets based on when you need the money. This ensures you never have to sell equity investments during a market downturn.

🪣 Bucket 1 (Liquid)
Holds 5 years of living expenses in ultra-safe instruments (e.g., Liquid Funds, FDs). You spend directly from here.
🪣 Bucket 2 (Balanced)
Holds another 5 years of expenses. Moderate risk/return (e.g., Balanced Advantage Funds) acting as a growth bridge.
🪣 Bucket 3 (Equity)
Holds the remainder of your wealth. High-growth Equity to beat long-term inflation. Has a 10+ year time horizon to recover from dips.

The "Waterfall" Refill Mechanism

  • Every 4 years, profits are siphoned from Bucket 2 (Balanced) to completely refill Bucket 1 (Liquid).
  • One year later (Year 5, 9, 13...), profits from Bucket 3 (Equity) are siphoned to refill Bucket 2.
  • A separate 3-year Emergency Fund sits completely untouched, acting as a final safety net for medical shocks.
