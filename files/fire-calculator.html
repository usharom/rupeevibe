🔥 FIRE Calculator

Financial Independence, Retire Early (FIRE) is the milestone where your invested money generates enough returns to cover your daily living expenses forever. Once you hit this “Crossover Point,” working for a salary becomes completely optional.

In India, planning for early retirement requires carefully balancing high inflation against your investment growth. This calculator runs a year-by-year simulation to find your exact FIRE number—the total corpus you need to safely quit the rat race.

How to use this tool
  1. Choose your mode Click Find My FIRE Year if you want the calculator to figure out exactly when you can quit working based on your current savings rate. Click Check a Target Year if you have a specific deadline in mind (like 2035) and want to see if you will successfully hit your target by then.
  2. Enter your baseline numbers Input your current monthly living expenses, how much money you actively save and invest each month, and your total invested corpus today.
  3. Set your market expectations Define your expected portfolio return (e.g., 10–12% for equity-heavy portfolios) and a realistic long-term inflation rate (typically 6–7% in India).
  4. Set your Safe Withdrawal Rate (SWR) This is the percentage of your total corpus you plan to withdraw each year to live on. A standard conservative estimate in India is between 3% and 4%.
