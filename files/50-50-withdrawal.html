⚖️ 50:50 Portfolio Rebalancing Tool

Protect your retirement corpus with disciplined rebalancing. A 50:50 strategy perfectly balances the high-growth engine of Equity with the steady, reliable anchor of Debt. But the real magic happens when the market rallies.

How to use this tool
  1. Choose your mode
    • Find Safe Withdrawal: Find a monthly amount that funds your target years without emergency refills or direct equity withdrawals under your assumptions.
    • Find Years to Depletion: Test a chosen withdrawal with emergency refills available.
    • Nifty Mar 2006–Mar 2026: Replay the supplied 20 March-to-March returns with your chosen withdrawal.
  2. Enter your portfolio details

    Enter your starting corpus, then choose a target duration or starting monthly withdrawal for your selected mode.

    The portfolio starts with 50% in equity and 50% in debt.

  3. Set your market assumptions

    Set equity return, debt return, and inflation. The Nifty tab replaces the fixed equity return with the supplied annual returns.

    Each year's withdrawal is taken before growth, from debt first. In manual and historical simulations, equity covers any shortfall. Withdrawals rise with inflation; monthly income is the annual withdrawal divided by 12.

  4. Understand the rebalancing rules
    • Spending buffer comes first After growth, if equity has grown and exceeds 65%, a full 50:50 rebalance takes priority when it also leaves at least two years of withdrawals in debt. Otherwise, debt below twice the current annual withdrawal is topped up from available equity. Refills bypass allocation thresholds and cooldowns, target the spending buffer, and do not reset the rebalance timer. Safe-withdrawal plans must never need this refill.
    • Standard rebalance: above 60% With the buffer funded, restore 50:50 when equity exceeds 60%, its value is higher than at the start of the period, and at least three years have passed since the last rebalance.
    • Book more profits when markets rise strongly In a bull market, your equity investments can grow quickly. If equity becomes more than 65% of your portfolio and moving back to 50:50 covers your spending reserve, the calculator sells some equity and moves the money into debt, bringing both back to 50% each. This books profits sooner, without waiting three years. Equity must also be worth more than at the start of the period. A higher percentage caused only by debt withdrawals does not qualify.

    The first move back to 50:50 can happen in any year. After each such move, the three-year wait starts again, unless the above-65% rule applies. The table shows your balances after money has been moved; the Action column explains what happened.

First, open Find Safe Withdrawal, set the target to 20 years, and calculate your monthly withdrawal.
