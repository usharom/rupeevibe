⚖️ 50:50 Portfolio Rebalancing Tool

Protect your retirement corpus with disciplined rebalancing. A 50:50 strategy perfectly balances the high-growth engine of Equity with the steady, reliable anchor of Debt. But the real magic happens when the market rallies.

How to use this tool
  1. Choose your mode Select Find Safe Withdrawal to calculate a sustainable monthly amount, or Find Years to Depletion to test how long a chosen withdrawal may last.
  2. Enter your portfolio details Add your total starting corpus and either your target retirement duration or current monthly withdrawal, depending on the selected mode.
  3. Set your market assumptions Enter expected equity and debt returns along with inflation. Withdrawals automatically increase each year to preserve purchasing power.
  4. Review automatic rebalancing The portfolio starts at 50% equity and 50% debt. When equity exceeds 60%, gains are swept into debt and the portfolio returns to a 50:50 allocation.
